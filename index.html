What We Do

Stucco & Exterior Plastering Services

Stucco Repair & Patching

Window and door patches, wall repairs, damaged stucco restoration, and careful texture matching.

Lath, Scratch & Brown

Paper, wire, lath, corner accessories, scratch coat, brown coat, and complete traditional stucco systems.

Acrylic & Smooth Finishes

Fine sand, smooth, Santa Barbara-style, and acrylic finishes for a clean, uniform exterior appearance.

Base Coat & Mesh

Fiberglass mesh reinforcement and base coat systems that help improve surface uniformity and reduce hairline cracking.

Exterior Restoration

Pressure washing, bonding preparation, re-stucco work, and exterior resurfacing for existing homes and buildings.

Residential & Commercial

From single-family homes to larger commercial and hospitality projects, we handle small repairs and full exterior scopes.
